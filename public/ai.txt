## Core Pillars

### 1. Major Sports Events
- **GBC 500 (Gravel Bikepacking Challenge 500)**:
  - Premiere gravel bike & bikepacking endurance race in Quebec.
  - Formats: 500 km (flagship autonomy route), 250 km (semi-bikepacking), 100 km (gravel day challenge).
  - Region: Eastern Townships & Mont Mégantic International Dark Sky Reserve.
  - Partners: Argon 18, Mont Orford.
  - Features: Live satellite GPS tracking, certified GPX tracks, official checkpoints & bivouacs.
  - URL: /evenements/gbc500

- **Trail des Neiges (Mont-Orford)**:
  - Legendary winter snow trail race at Mount Orford.
  - Formats: 5 km (discovery), 10 km (intermediate), 15 km (expert night trail with headlamps).
  - URL: /evenements/trail-des-neiges

- **Raids Aventure Multisports**:
  - Raid International Gaspésie (RIG): 4-day expedition linking the ocean to the Chic-Chocs summits (sea kayak, mountain bike, cliff rappel, night navigation).
  - Raid Témiscamingue: Wilderness boreal navigation in canoes and forest trek.
  - URL: /evenements/raids

### 2. Corporate & B2B Services
- **Turnkey Sports Event Management**:
  - Trail and course design with GPS mapping and environmental authorizations.
  - Field logistics, ephemeral bivouac camps, and fluid/food provisioning.
  - Complete safety and emergency civil coordination with specialized medical teams.
  - Electronic timing (RFID chips) and live interactive leaderboard.
  - URL: /services/organisation-evenements

- **Extreme Television & Video Production**:
  - 4K / HDR all-terrain broadcast filming in hostile weather and remote environments.
  - Certified drone pilots for mountain and aquatic coverage.
  - Embedded sports camera operators on foot, ski, kayak, and bike.
  - National TV documentaries and daily social media video clips.
  - URL: /services/production-tele
